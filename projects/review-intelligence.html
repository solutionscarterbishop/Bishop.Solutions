Overview

Review Intelligence is a purpose-built analytics platform for multi-location operators. It ingests customer feedback across sources, runs sentiment and complaint classification, and turns the results into decisions at the level operators actually care about — this location, this shift, this problem, this week.

Problem

Multi-location businesses are drowning in review volume and starved of insight. Aggregate star ratings hide operational rot. Location-level dashboards are backwards-looking. Nobody flags the specific complaint category that just spiked in one city, or the location that quietly turned around. Operators need answers, not more visualizations.

Approach

Treat reviews as time-series operational signal, not marketing content. Classify at the sentence level, roll up to review, location, region, and brand. Detect movement, not just averages. Surface the small number of things a district manager can actually do this week.

Architecture

Ingestion pipeline pulls reviews across sources into a normalized store. A processing layer runs language models for sentiment and complaint classification, deduplication, spam filtering, and PII redaction. A dimensional model powers cross-cutting queries by location, brand, region, category, and time. A lightweight web app exposes it all to non-technical operators.

Technology

Cloud-native data pipeline with a columnar analytics store, an LLM-assisted classifier stack, and a modern web frontend. Cost per review is engineered down through batched processing and cache-aware model routing so scaling to hundreds of thousands of reviews stays boring.

Features

Sentiment analysis (sentence-level)
Complaint classification taxonomy
Location comparison & benchmarking
Regional & brand rollups
Competitive insight views
Revenue opportunity discovery
Operational anomaly detection
Trend & movement alerts
Executive summary reports
Location action briefs

Lessons

  • Averages lie. The most valuable insights live in the movement of a single category at a single location over a two-week window.
  • Taxonomy is the product. The complaint categories you commit to shape every downstream decision.
  • Operators want briefs, not dashboards. A one-page "what changed and what to do" beats a beautiful chart nobody opens.

Impact

Reduced the effective distance between customer feedback and operational response from weeks to days. Operators use the platform to prioritize field visits, coach specific shifts, and stop small complaint categories before they metastasize.

Future direction

Predictive alerts on complaint categories before they cross a threshold, competitive benchmarking against local rivals, and automated draft responses that stay within brand voice and legal guardrails.

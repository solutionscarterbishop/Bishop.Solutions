Overview

Bishop Plans is a modern independent brokerage focused on clarity, trust, and long-term client relationships. It serves individuals and families across a multi-state footprint with Medicare, life, and supplemental products, backed by a technology stack designed by someone who lives in the workflow.

Problem

Insurance is bought once and lived with for decades, and yet most consumers describe the experience as opaque, high-pressure, and paperwork-heavy. Most brokerages either optimize for volume (bad for the client) or for a very narrow product line (bad for coverage fit). Both leave the client under-served.

Approach

Treat the client conversation as the product. Match to the right carrier and product across a wide appointment portfolio. Use technology to remove paperwork friction, not to replace the human. Follow through — Medicare and life clients need service years after the application is signed.

Architecture

Operationally, Bishop Plans runs on the same internal platforms featured elsewhere on this site: Bishop CRM for pipeline, communication, compliance, and commissions; Marketing Command Center for outreach and compliance-safe content; Review Intelligence-adjacent tooling for feedback loops.

Technology

Quoting, e-application, e-signature, and enrollment tools sit behind an operator-first interface. Everything a client interaction produces — call recording, message trail, document, decision — is preserved and searchable so service years later stays as good as service on day one.

Lessons

  • Trust is the moat. A client who trusts the agent renews, refers, and comes back for other lines.
  • Compliance discipline is a competitive advantage. Fewer surprises, fewer regulatory issues, more time in the game.
  • Product breadth serves the client. Appointment coverage across 40+ carriers means "the right plan" is a genuine option, not the plan that pays the most.

Impact

Serves clients across the entire continental U.S. with a service quality typically associated with a single-agent local shop. The internal tooling makes the operation scale without diluting the client experience.

Future direction

Expanded life and annuity lines, deeper post-enrollment service tooling, and a slow, deliberate broadening of the agent bench inside a supported, tech-forward environment.

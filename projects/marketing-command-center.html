Overview

Marketing Command Center is the connective tissue between the calendar, the creative, the approvers, the channels, the compliance officer, and the reports. It replaces the collage of docs, sheets, chats, and cloud folders most marketing teams live inside.

Problem

Real marketing work is a chain of hand-offs — brief, draft, review, revision, approval, schedule, publish, monitor, iterate — and every hand-off breaks in a different tool. Compliance in regulated verticals (like insurance) makes the chain more brittle, not less. Traditional marketing suites optimize for one link at a time and leave the seams to the humans.

Approach

Model the campaign as a first-class object with a lifecycle and a paper trail. Every asset, message, and decision attaches to it. Approvals live where the work lives, not in email. Publishing pushes directly into the channels the team already uses. Reporting closes the loop by pulling performance back onto the campaign record itself.

Architecture

Central campaign object with linked assets, tasks, approvals, and publish events. Integrations with content channels, ad platforms, and compliance tooling sit behind an adapter layer so channel changes don't ripple through the app. A real-time collaboration layer keeps the campaign kanban live for distributed teams.

Technology

Typed web application with a document-based content store, workflow engine for approvals, and event-driven publishing. Fine-grained roles and audit logs make regulated verticals safe to run inside a single tool.

Lessons

  • Approvals are the choke point. Whichever tool owns approvals wins the team's daily attention.
  • Compliance is UX. Making the compliant path easier than the risky path is more effective than any policy document.
  • Assets outlive campaigns. The library needs to be first-class from day one, not bolted on.

Impact

Cut the cycle time from brief to published asset by consolidating the workflow into one system. Compliance became a lived-in habit rather than a periodic emergency. Reporting stopped being a monthly exercise.

Future direction

AI-assisted drafting inside the approval loop, richer performance attribution across paid and organic surfaces, and templated campaign kits for common regulated categories.

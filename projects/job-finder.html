Overview

Job Finder is the tool you'd build if you took a job search seriously: an aggregator that respects the candidate's time, a scoring model that reflects reality, and a workflow that actually gets the application in front of a human.

Problem

General-purpose job boards optimize for engagement, not fit. Filtering is coarse, ranking is opaque, and applying is repetitive. The good roles show up in a dozen places at once; the bad roles show up in a hundred. Candidates burn hours filtering through noise before they can even think about applying well.

Approach

Ingest broadly, dedupe aggressively, score against candidate qualification rather than keyword match, and rank by opportunity quality. Turn the top of the ranked list into an application-ready queue. Learn from what actually gets responses.

Architecture

Ingestion workers pull listings across sources into a normalized store with fingerprint-based dedupe. A scoring service compares candidate profiles to listings with an interpretable model. A workflow layer orchestrates outreach, application drafting, and response tracking.

Technology

Typed web app with async workers for ingestion and scoring, a relational store for listings and applications, and an embedded LLM layer for draft generation and semantic match. Rate-limiting and politeness are built into every source integration.

Lessons

  • Interpretable ranking wins. Candidates trust — and act on — scores they can understand and adjust.
  • Deduplication is the unglamorous secret. Cleaning listing noise unlocks every downstream feature.
  • The application step is where drop-off lives. Removing friction there matters more than adding another data source.

Impact

Compresses hours of daily job-search grinding into a focused review of a curated shortlist. Time-per-application drops as the tool takes on the boilerplate work, freeing candidates to focus on the parts of an application that actually differentiate.

Future direction

Deeper career intelligence — market compensation, company signal, and role trajectory — plus opt-in coaching that turns response data into a live playbook.

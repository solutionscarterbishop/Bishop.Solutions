Overview

Fourx is a personal research platform for trading systems. It exists to make the cost of testing an idea near-zero, so that the quality of ideas — not the friction of infrastructure — becomes the bottleneck. It is deliberately research-first: no black-box performance chase, no shortcuts.

Problem

Most retail-facing trading tools are optimized for engagement, not for honest research. Backtests are optimistic, data quality is opaque, and strategy comparison is inconsistent. It's easy to build something that looks like a strategy and hard to build something worth funding.

Approach

Start with a clean, versioned data layer. Enforce look-ahead safety by construction. Make every strategy a first-class object with a reproducible run manifest. Compare fairly. Kill ideas quickly. Save the ones that survive scrutiny.

Architecture

Immutable time-series data lake, feature engineering layer with typed contracts, strategy engine with deterministic runs, and an evaluation module that computes risk-aware metrics. Everything writes to a run store, so any result can be reproduced and diffed against its neighbors.

Technology

Python-heavy research stack with vectorized backtests, containerized runs, and analytics notebooks that pull from the run store. Data quality checks live at ingest time; every downstream consumer trusts the upstream contract.

Features

Historical data lake & versioning
Feature engineering with typed contracts
Strategy definition & reproducible runs
Look-ahead safety enforcement
Risk-aware performance metrics
Strategy comparison & ablations
Analytics notebooks & reports
Run store & artifact tracking

Lessons

  • Data quality is the strategy. The cleanest, most-versioned dataset beats the cleverest model on dirty data.
  • Reproducibility is a feature. Any result you can't reproduce is a result you can't defend.
  • Discipline beats excitement. Kill ideas fast and cheaply; keep the ones that keep surviving.

Impact

Turned a series of half-tested ideas into a disciplined research workflow. Individual strategies come and go; the discipline is the durable output.

Future direction

Broader asset coverage, sharper feature engineering primitives, and public writeups of the research process itself.

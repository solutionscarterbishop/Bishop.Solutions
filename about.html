The Founder

Carter Bishop.
Builder first, everything else second.

I'm 20 years old, based in Palm Harbor, Florida, and I've already built an enterprise SaaS intelligence platform for a national restaurant chain, scaled a TikTok creator from 45,000 to 1.6 million followers, and spent years selling regulated financial products in high-pressure environments. I didn't follow the traditional path. I built things instead.

My career started in food service and retail — running shifts at Panera, selling suits at Men's Wearhouse. Then I moved into health insurance, where I learned how to advise people on complex decisions under strict compliance requirements. That's where I sharpened the skill that matters most: earning trust fast and delivering real value under pressure.

Along the way, I took a creator brand from 45K to 1.6 million followers — building the growth systems, content strategy, branded website, logo, and apparel catalog end-to-end. Then I built Bishop Solutions and shipped Review Insights: an AI-powered review intelligence platform analyzing 250+ restaurant locations across 49 states. I don't just talk about building. I ship.

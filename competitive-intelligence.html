Competitive Intelligence

Know the market
before you enter it.

Expanding into new territories without visibility into what competitors are doing well — and failing at — is expensive guesswork. We replace that guesswork with data.

The Problem

You're entering markets blind.
Your competitors aren't.

When you expand into a new city or region, you're betting on assumptions. You don't know what customers hate about the existing options. You don't know where the service gaps are. You don't know which neighborhoods are underserved. Your competitors already have this intelligence — because their customers are telling them every day through reviews. We just read what they're saying.

The Solution

Turn their reviews into your strategy.

We mine competitor reviews across your target markets and build a complete competitive landscape — so you know exactly where to win before you open the doors.

Competitor Review Analysis

Systematic mining of competitor reviews to surface operational weaknesses, recurring complaints, and service breakdowns by location.

Market Gap Identification

Find the underserved needs in every target geography. What are customers asking for that nobody is delivering? That's your opening.

Expansion Intelligence

Data-driven market entry recommendations. We tell you where to go, what to prioritize, and how to position against the incumbents.

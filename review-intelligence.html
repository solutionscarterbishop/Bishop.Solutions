The Problem

Your reviews are a goldmine.
Nobody's mining them.

Restaurant chains with hundreds of locations generate thousands of Google Reviews every month. Your teams can't read them all. Star ratings tell you almost nothing actionable. Complaint patterns go undetected, revenue leaks compound silently, and the customers telling you exactly what's wrong get ignored — buried in a sea of data nobody has time to process.

The Solution

Every review, classified and scored.

We build AI-powered pipelines that ingest, classify, and analyze every customer review across all your locations — automatically, continuously, and at scale.

AI Classification Engine

Automated complaint categorization using local LLM inference. Every review sorted into actionable categories — food quality, order accuracy, service speed, cleanliness, and more.

Store-Level Dashboards

Individual performance views for every location. Trend tracking, complaint patterns, improvement scoring, and regional benchmarking — all in one place.

Revenue Impact Scoring
